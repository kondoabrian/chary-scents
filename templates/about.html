Welcome To Chary Scents

Chary Scents is a beauty store located in Bunga, along Ggaba Road, Kampala, Uganda.

We provide perfumes, fragrance oils, lip gloss, lipsticks, makeup products, skincare products, beauty gift sets and accessories.

Whether you are shopping for yourself or looking for a beautiful gift, Chary Scents provides a variety of beauty products to choose from.
